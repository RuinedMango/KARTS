## Third-party assets

No third-party textures, models, or audio are currently included.

When adding an asset, record:

- File(s)
- Author
- Source URL
- License
- Changes made

Do not add assets whose license is incompatible with the project's asset
license. Third-party assets remain under their original licenses and are not
covered by `LICENSE-ASSETS`.
